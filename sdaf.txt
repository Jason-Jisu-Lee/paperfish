Paperfish — project brief

Genre: idle/incremental hybrid. Setting: single-screen deep-sea ocean, descending through zones.

Core loop

Start with one fish. Fish generate currency passively.
A fish's income scales with its age, up to a cap.
Each fish type has its own age/income curve. Curves differ in shape — some pay little for a long stretch, then spike. Average income rises with age, with occasional dips. The starting fish uses a flat curve as a baseline.
At growth stages, the player chooses traits (placeholder upgrades for now)



Traits are a second decision layer, chosen periodically.
 Each type has a population cap; upgrades can raise it. Currency unlocks new types.


Zone progression

Deeper zones require purchasing pressure-rated gear with a large lump sum — a save-versus-spend decision, not a passive threshold.
Each zone uses its own currency. On descending, accumulated income converts into different currency with different ratio (undecided details)



Prestige

Prestige resets all zones and returns the player to zone 1, with permanent bonuses.




Tone and pacing

Medium pace. Always something for the player to do.
Opens as lighthearted deep-sea exploration, then gradually turns unsettling. We'll add a story to this later. The player is the "diver" who's down in the ocean to explore and help fish grow.

core thing in this game is a sense of progression and progressive introduction of new upgrades/mechanics, etc.

none of this is a hard rule and we'll adjust as we go and test.
